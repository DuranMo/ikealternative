# IKEAlternative

IKEA smart light control from your web browser.

I found that the IKEA Home app was particularly hard to use for gradual light changes (specifically with the gradual hue/temperature change features). So, I decided to make IKEAlternative, a simple way to control your smart lights that allows you to be really specific about your scenes, making it easier to stop your lights from randomly turning green in the middle of the night.

## Features

A wide variety of light control options! In particular, this app lets you specify the **exact brightness**, **temperature**, and **color** you want instead of having to move a slide around until you find the one that's just right.

Scenes can be created on the website, and will be promptly saved to the hub and synced onto the IKEA Home app. We currently offer fixed brightness, fade over time, and daily on/off scene customization, with more RGB-based animated scenes to come.

Scheduled routines and fades run on your hub, so you can close the website after setting them up.

## Get started

You'll need an IKEA DIRIGERA hub and compatible lights, with your computer connected to the same home network.

Follow the [installation guide for Mac and Windows](INSTALLATION.md), then open the dashboard in your browser. You can also try it with simulated lights before connecting your own.
